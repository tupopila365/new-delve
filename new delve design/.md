# Delve Traveler Homepage Specification

## 1. Product Purpose

Delve helps travelers discover and organize their entire trip in one place. Travelers can find stays, transport, food, activities, events, guides, shops, genuine deals, traveler-created Journeys, and visual recommendations from Delvers.

A new visitor must understand what Delve does within five seconds of landing.

### Core Messaging
- **Main Headline:** “Discover your whole trip in one place.”
- **Supporting Text:** “Find stays, transport, food, activities, and real deals. See journeys shared by travelers and plan what fits your time and budget.”
- **Supporting Proof:** “Compare services · Direct provider contact · Transparent pricing · Learn from real traveler experiences.”
- **Primary Actions:**
  - Search a place
  - Explore nearby
  - Filter by service

> **Direct Connection & Discovery Model (No Intermediary Booking Features):**  
> Delve operates as a discovery and direct-connection platform. Users explore verified services, view transparent pricing, compare local and resident rates, and connect directly with verified businesses or operators. There are no intermediary checkout flows, hidden booking commissions, or reservation paywalls.

The homepage showcases the full suite of 11 Delve service categories with dedicated icons, descriptive labels, and live filters:

1. **Stays** (Hotels, desert chalets, lodges, campsites, guest houses)

## 4. Homepage Section Order & Architecture

### 1. Hero & Discovery
- Value headline and supporting copy.
- Interactive Destination Selector (Swakopmund, Windhoek, Walvis Bay, Sossusvlei, Etosha) updating content reactively.
- Quick search bar with instant typeahead.
- Mood shortcut chips: *Weekend away*, *Coast & Dunes*, *Wildlife Safaris*, *Family Friendly*, *Easy on the wallet*, *Evenings out*.
- Trust proof points: *Verified local providers · Direct operator contact · Transparent pricing*.

### 2. Explore All Services
- 11 service category tiles with Lucide icons, descriptions, and item counts.
- Clicking any tile filters the featured services catalog or navigates to that category view.

### 3. Featured Services Showcase (Backend-Connected)
- Connected to `/api/v2/listings/public` via `listingClient.ts`.
- Filterable by category pills (*All*, *Stays*, *Activities*, *Food & Drink*, *Guides*, *Shops*).
- Cards show: Cover photo, service category badge, business name, verified badge, location, transparent price basis (e.g. `N$ 450/person`, `N$ 1,200/night`), rating, review count, save button, and "View Details" button.

### 4. Deals For This Place
- Connected to `/api/v2/deals/public` via `dealClient.ts`.
- Deal cards show: Photo, discount chip (e.g. `20% OFF`, `Local Rate`), business name, destination, current price/saving, validity period, save button, and "View Deal Details" button.

### 5. Transport Discovery
- Multi-modal transport rail covering:
  - Rent a vehicle (`N$ 700/day`)
  - Private ride with driver (`N$ 900/transfer`)
  - Community ride (`N$ 240/seat`)
  - Bus or minibus (`N$ 280/seat`)
  - Airport transfer (`N$ 450/trip`)
- Explicit price units and operator verification badges.
- Action: "View Route & Operator" (no checkout booking).

### 6. Journeys to Borrow
- Connected to `/api/v2/journeys` via `journeyClient.ts`.
- Route chips (e.g., `Windhoek → Swakopmund → Walvis Bay`), duration, number of stops, transport modes.
- Explicit label: *"Historical trip cost: N$ 3,800 (What this traveler spent — non-bookable itinerary)"*.
- Action: "Explore Journey".

### 7. Easy on the Wallet
- Budget-conscious services and Journeys with clear explanations (*Under N$ 500*, *Local rate available*, *Free self-guided*).

### 8. From Delvers
- Visual community travel stories and photos with creator handles, places, likes, and save actions.

### 9. Ask Locals
- Real travel Q&A cards with questions, place tags, author, and accepted answers from verified locals.

### 10. Trust & Local Platform Guidance
- Explanations of verified businesses, local resident rates, direct operator contact, and safety guidelines.

### 11. Footer
- Links to About Delve, Service directory, Safety, Guidelines, List your business, Currency selector, and Region switcher.

## 5. Backend Integration & Data Resilience

- **Authoritative Backend:**
  - Listings: `GET /api/v2/listings/public`
  - Deals: `GET /api/v2/deals/public`
  - Journeys: `GET /api/v2/journeys`
- **Graceful Fallback:** If backend data is unavailable or database tables are unseeded, the homepage automatically falls back to curated high-fidelity showcase data from the data layer so the UI remains complete, interactive, and visually impressive at all times.
- **Independent Section Loading:** Each section loads independently with its own skeleton state so a delay in one query does not block the rest of the page.
