## Issues Found

### Issue 1: Fixed mobile navigation can visually overlap mid-page content

- **What**: The persistent bottom navigation floats over whichever content is currently behind it, including helper text near the search-to-discovery transition.
- **Where**: Mobile viewport, fixed bottom navigation.
- **Why it matters**: This is a minor readability interruption during scrolling, although content remains reachable and the page has bottom clearance.
- **Suggested fix**: Consider an opaque safe-area scrim or hide/reduce the bar while the user is actively scrolling and restore it when scrolling stops.

### Issue 2: Discovery cards still use prominent promotional price decoration

- **What**: The undated Freesia card retains a struck-through static price before the discounted nightly price.
- **Where**: Pre-date discovery result cards.
- **Why it matters**: It adds a small amount of visual competition beside the deliberately restrained “Add dates to check availability” message.
- **Suggested fix**: Keep the discount treatment typographically quieter or move promotional detail to the property page once availability dates are known.
